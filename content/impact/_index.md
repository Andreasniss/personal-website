My work connects enterprise AI decisions with the engineering evidence needed to make them. I bring more than 15 years across technology, architecture, and customer-facing delivery, with a current focus on applied and agentic AI.

The public record below covers reference systems and teaching. It describes my professional scope without attributing confidential customer results or unverified commercial outcomes to these projects.

## Public engineering evidence

| Work | Decision I owned | What a reviewer can inspect |
|---|---|---|
| [Runbook Relay](/projects/runbook-relay/) | Move approval and execution state to a durable server boundary | A live blocked-action proof, scoped approvals, replay behavior, receipts, and the source |
| [7DayFocus AI Delivery Lab](/projects/7dayfocus-ai-delivery-lab/) | Keep AI output proposal-only and validate the complete change before approval | Domain rules, stale-state rejection, deterministic proposal cases, and delivery artifacts |
| [Agent Reliability Lab](/projects/mistral-playground/) | Separate model calls from bounded tool execution and operational telemetry | Credential-free preview, allow-listed tools, retry limits, privacy tests, and evaluation fixtures |

The dated repository snapshots record 30, 244, and 26 automated tests respectively. They also define 50, 24, and six evaluation cases. These counts describe the evidence available to inspect, not adoption, business impact, or production scale. Runbook Relay's 50-task live-model suite has no published result; the other cases exercise deterministic application behavior. Some evaluation cases also run within automated test suites, so these are not additive measures of coverage.

I own intent, architecture, requirements, evaluation criteria, risk, and release decisions, and review merged work. AI tools assist with implementation and documentation. The project pages link the tests and explain their limits.

## Enterprise AI delivery

In my AWS role, I work with organizations adopting AI in regulated environments. I help define the workflow and success criteria, evaluate model and system behavior, design the architecture, and identify the security, compliance, operational, and partner-delivery work required to proceed.

The useful output is a decision the team can act on: proceed with a bounded proof of value, change the design, close an evidence gap, or stop an approach that does not meet the need. Public labs demonstrate selected engineering patterns from this broader discipline. They are independent of customer implementations.

## Product judgment

I completed 7DayFocus as a learning lab and stopped pursuing it as a standalone product when existing task tools connected to agents met my personal need more directly. Keeping the verified implementation while stopping further product investment was part of the result.
